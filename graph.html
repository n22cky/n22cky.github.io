Craftsmanship

Technique proficiency; skill application.

Uniqueness

When an object is the first of its kind; has pushed its respective medium forward.

Cultural value

How an object is perceived/valued by one's own culture or society.

Note on external factors, such as subjective perspective and beauty

When rating for an honest assessment, attempt to remove bias and observe the work for objective truth. Be open to changing a rating upon receiving new information. The perceived beauty of an object is not a relevant factor in this framework.
